  - title: Many render styles
    details: Cartoon, tube, trace, lines, points, spheres, balls and sticks, licorice and molecular surfaces, with order-independent transparency.
  - title: Made for structural biology
    details: mmCIF with biological assemblies, auth and label numbering, glycans as 3D-SNFG symbols, nucleic-acid bases as filled rings.
  - title: Easy to embed
    details: A few lines put a structure on a page. Picking, hover and selection highlighting, and your own meshes and labels are built in.
